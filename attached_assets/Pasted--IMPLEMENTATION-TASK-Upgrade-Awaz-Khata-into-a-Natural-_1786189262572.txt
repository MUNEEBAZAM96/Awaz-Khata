# 🧠 IMPORTANT ARCHITECTURE RULE

The LLM must **NOT calculate financial numbers**.

Groq is responsible only for:

```text
UNDERSTANDING USER INTENT
```

The finance engine is responsible for:

```text
ALL FINANCIAL CALCULATIONS
```

The response generator is responsible for:

```text
TURNING VERIFIED FINANCE RESULTS
INTO NATURAL URDU
```

Uplift AI is responsible for:

```text
VOICE INPUT + VOICE OUTPUT
```

Therefore:

```text
Uplift AI
   ↓
Speech Recognition

Groq
   ↓
Intent Understanding

Finance Engine
   ↓
Truth / Mathematics

Response Generator
   ↓
Natural Urdu

Uplift AI
   ↓
Speech Output
```

Do not violate this architecture.
